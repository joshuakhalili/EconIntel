## Synthesis of the fourteen high-risk interpretations

- **deflator-direction:** BEA's nominal/price identity supports the conditional direction, not a measured AI price decline. A falling matched price raises real growth relative to nominal growth; depreciation and asset coverage still prevent an inference about capital deepening.

- **capital-deepening:** BLS defines capital deepening in terms of capital input relative to labour. BEA's investment-flow construction complements that distinction: a spending increase can coexist with depreciation or faster labour growth. Neither establishes an AI productivity return.

- **orders-deflation:** The BEA identity supports the need for a matched deflator; it does not supply one for Census electronic-product orders. The nominal historical comparison remains descriptive, and both directions of real change remain possible.

- **ulc-definition:** BLS directly defines compensation per unit of output. The cloud-PPI methodology concerns a different service basket; comparing their percentage changes does not put labour hours and AI computations on an equivalent basis.

- **ulc-and-productivity:** BLS's ratio identity permits both series to rise. Wage growth must be reconstructed multiplicatively from matched compensation, hours and output measures; a simple difference between two index-growth rates is not the wage measure.

- **capital-contributions:** BLS distinguishes capital-input and labour-productivity accounting. This supports checking different denominators, but the incompatibility of these particular OECD series still requires their own codebook. No numerical return ratio is endorsed.

- **publication-effective:** NARA directly distinguishes publication from effective dates and exceptions. This challenges the earlier 'rules in force' caption, but does not establish the legal status of any individual sampled document.

- **proposal-cohorts:** NARA describes the rulemaking sequence; a proposal-to-final-rule conversion estimate additionally requires linked actions and follow-up time. This is a statistical deduction, not a conversion rate estimated by the tutorial.

- **capacity-status:** Epoch explicitly permits planned systems and describes uncertainty. IEA's geographic and connection constraints further explain why announced power and deliverable operating supply differ. Neither source validates the status of each ingested cluster.

- **hosting-basket:** BLS's cloud study identifies basket and quality-adjustment limitations. Its microprocessor study independently illustrates that hardware quality adjustment is not interchangeable with hosting-service prices. No AI-service inflation estimate is established.

- **workplace-complementarity:** The AI-at-Work v2 abstract reports heterogeneous productivity gains in a specific workplace. Acemoglu's task framework makes aggregate effects depend on affected task shares and substitution versus complementarity; positive workplace results therefore do not settle entry-level hiring or economy-wide employment.

- **jcurve-not-guaranteed:** The J-curve manuscript expressly rejects guaranteed payoffs. Acemoglu's framework additionally conditions aggregate gains on task exposure and savings; workplace productivity evidence establishes possibility in one setting, not the size or timing of a national productivity acceleration.

- **financing-versus-use:** The SEC's separation of operating, investing and financing cash flows directly challenges the earlier assertion that capex necessarily comes from operations. Aggregate investment and venture funding describe different uses and channels, not a reconciled funding account.

- **band-composition:** Eurostat's consumption-weighted aggregation makes composition a concrete rival explanation to bargaining power. The observed band gap can describe average prices, but identifying a volume discount requires matched contracts or a design controlling for composition.

Run `node scripts/check-editorial-completeness.js` for the checked-in snapshot, or run the same script with `--database` through the isolated-staging wrapper. The gate rejects changed prose, uncovered or overlapping spans, changed activation, missing/extra points, changed values/labels/basis/qualifiers, figure/source changes, invalid or missing64-hex PDF hashes, duplicate source IDs, changed lens/readings/tickers and unresolved records without attempts. It checks source-transcribed values on qualified numeric points as well as point counts. Mutation tests deliberately keep counts unchanged.

The gate cannot prove that an agent's economic reasoning is correct, that a publication's data are unbiased, or that a source PDF has not been replaced upstream. It verifies exact assessed content and requires explicit dispositions. Discovery, source refresh, append-only research reviews, provider operations, country coverage, deployment recovery and publication are covered by the other intervention workstreams, not silently certified here.
