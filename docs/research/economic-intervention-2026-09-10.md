## Synthesis principles applied

Economic questions now separate the measured object from the desired causal answer. Sector shares do not cancel heterogeneous shocks. Nominal investment cannot establish real resource growth without prices; capital-stock contributions and labour-productivity contributions cannot stand in for a matched return calculation. A hosting basket is not useful AI output. The absence of an aggregate signal cannot establish a zero effect without a counterfactual and a detection threshold.

The task-based literature motivates both substitution and complementarity; the workplace abstract supports possible worker-level benefits but cannot settle hiring. The J-curve provides a conditional explanation for delayed measured gains and explicitly does not guarantee them. These are different estimands and mechanisms, not votes to average into one percentage.

Publication status, source access, method and uncertainty stay attached to evidence. Earlier paper vintages remain distinguishable from later findings. New quantitative or causal assertions require their own locators and reproducible calculations before the draft can be called verified.
